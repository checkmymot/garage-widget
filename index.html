Questions garages ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The MOT data is public DVSA data and the checker on our site is free, so the widget is free too — no trial, no catch, no "pro" tier.

Will it break my website's design?

No. The widget renders in an isolated container with its own styles — it can't inherit or leak your theme's CSS.

Can I change the colours or text?

Not in v1 — it's deliberately zero-config so it works everywhere. If enough garages ask, we'll add theme options.

Do you collect my customers' data?

No. The widget sends nothing anywhere until the visitor clicks through to the checker on checkmymot.com — exactly as if they'd visited us directly.

Can I remove it any time?

Delete the two lines. Gone instantly, nothing left behind.
